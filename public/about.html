Our story

Making cleaning
safe, fair, and effortless.

One Stop Cleaner is one place where families book verified cleaners, cleaning professionals find steady work, and brands reach the homes that need them. We built it to feel effortless and safe from day one.

Our mission

Connect people with reliable, verified cleaning.

Transparent pricing, secure payments, and peace of mind on every booking. We match families with verified cleaners and give professionals the work they deserve.

Our vision

The most trusted marketplace — for every home and every cleaner.

Cleaners connected, protected, and fairly rewarded. Families trusted. Brands reaching the right homes.

What we stand for

Our values

Trust first

Every verification, payment, and review exists to make you feel safe.

Fairness for all

Cleaners get paid fairly and on time. Clients get honest pricing. We design for both sides, always.

Simple by design

Complex problems deserve simple solutions. We strip away friction so cleaning feels effortless.

The escrow advantage

Your money is safe until the job is done right.

Every payment flows through secure escrow. We hold your funds, never the cleaner — so there's no risk, no surprises, and full peace of mind.

1. Pay into escrow

Secure your booking with a held payment. Your card is charged, but the funds are locked safely — never handed over upfront.

2. Cleaner does the job

A vetted, insured cleaner arrives on time and does the work. Track everything through your booking — no endless texting.

3. Approve & release

Only when you're satisfied do you release the funds. If anything's off, our 24/7 team resolves the dispute fairly.

Our Story

Redefining trust in
home maintenance.

It began with a singular vision: inviting a professional into your home should feel inherently safe and entirely seamless. We built One Stop Cleaner to be the definitive bridge between discerning families and the industry's most dedicated experts.

Mission

To connect people with reliable, verified cleaning services and the products they need — with transparent pricing, secure payments, and peace of mind on every drop-off.

Vision

To become the most trusted cleaning marketplace — where every home and every cleaning service is connected, protected, and fairly rewarded.

What we stand for

Our values

Trust first

Every verification, every payment, every review exists to make you feel safe. Trust is the foundation of everything we build.

Fairness for all

Cleaning services get paid fairly and on time. Clients get honest pricing. We design for both sides of the marketplace, always.

Simple by design

Complex problems deserve simple solutions. We strip away friction so getting your cleaning done feels effortless.
